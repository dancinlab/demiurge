## 1. The FTO problem statement (read first)

**WAY-316606 is a KNOWN compound** (PubChem CID 16727102, published SFRP1 pharmacology). A composition-of-matter claim on WAY-316606 *itself* is **not novel** — it is anticipated by the prior art. This is the central FTO/patentability constraint and it drives the entire strategy:

> **The patentable core is NOT the parent molecule — it is (a) the novel analogs (A1/A2/A3), (b) the method-of-use (SFRP1 inhibition for AGA via the topical-follicular route), and (c) the formulation + delivery (nanoemulsion / NANOBOT / WEAVE / siRNA / AAV).**

A method-of-use claim ("SFRP1 inhibition for treating AGA") can be valid over a known compound **if that specific therapeutic use is itself novel and non-obvious** — this is the standard generic-compound-new-use pattern. FTO to *practice* WAY-316606 (it is a research chemical, no composition patent blocking topical use is assumed but **must be cleared by a real freedom-to-operate search** — flagged) is distinct from patentability of AGA-RX's own claims.

Combined with the COM on the analogs, this is the **patentable core** the FTO statement points to.
