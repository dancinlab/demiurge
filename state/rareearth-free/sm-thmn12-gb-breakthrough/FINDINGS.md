# SM-THMN12-GB verdict (heavy-RE breakthrough core)
Verdict: OPEN-but-thermodynamically-handicapped. Sm-1-12 intrinsic H_A 10-12T (>Nd2Fe14B), bulk Hc plateau ~1.4-1.5T (~14% H_A).
Root: Sm-Fe has NO low-melting Sm-rich eutectic (all peritectic) — Nd-Fe-B's 640°C eutectic wets grains into non-FM film; Sm-1-12 lacks native analog + 1:12 metastable.
BUT Cu induces non-FM Fe-lean GB phase (SmCu CsCl + Sm-hcp) → thermodynamics doesn't forbid it. APT shows native GB still Fe-rich (arXiv:2410.06540).
Decisive missing: (i) continuous/wetting Fe-lean GB film around every grain (currently patchy) (ii) first-principles GB-wetting/stability map (nonexistent — our DFT+CALPHAD opportunity).
