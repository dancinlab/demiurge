# GB-WETTING-SCREEN verdict (DFT candidate ranking)
Top 3 Sm-X-Cu GB-phase candidates for DFT (priority): #1 Sm-Ga-Cu (novel bet — Cu+Ga each proven non-FM wetting IGP in real SmFe12, Ga lowest-melt accelerant); #2 SmCu B2 (~Sm50Cu50, 1.4-1.5T record anchor / method validation); #3 Sm-Cu-Al (Sm-Al eutectic 635°C, but Al dissolves into 1:12 risk).
Disqualified: Si (eutectic 1212°C > decomp), Co-Cu ((Cu,Co)5Sm ferromagnetic anti-pattern).
Nd benchmark: Nd-Cu eutectic 520°C, Nd80Ga15Cu5 best wetting.
Decisive uncertainty = continuous film vs islanding (discontinuous → FM bridges survive). DFT targets: γ_interface vs γ_grain (wetting), spin-resolved net moment (decoupling), Sm(Cu_xGa_{1-x}) mixing energy (continuity).
