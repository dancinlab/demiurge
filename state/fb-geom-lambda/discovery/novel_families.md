## Why off-diagonal/SSH matters (mechanism re-anchor)

- bond-SSH phonons modulate the HOPPING integral t → "off-diagonal" e-ph. In the bond-SSH bipolaron,
  the pair mass is only WEAKLY enhanced (light bipolaron), and large pair-HOPPING amplitude is generated —
  which aids phase coherence. The Tc bound for bond-SSH bipolaronic SC is EXPONENTIALLY larger than Holstein.
  (Sous/Berciu/Zhang "Light Bipolarons", arXiv:1805.06109; "Bipolaronic high-Tc SC", arXiv:2203.07380;
   "High-Tc SC induced by SSH e-ph", arXiv:2308.06222; semiclassical bond-modulated PRB 109 L220502.)
- This is EXACTLY the recipe demiurge already adopted. The grounding question: which REAL material families
  carry (a) a bond-stretch/bond-modulating phonon that is the dominant e-ph channel, (b) real carriers,
  (c) a flat or near-flat band so the geometric D_s ∝ ⟨g⟩ route is live, (d) underexplored for the geometric lens.

## RANKED NOVEL FAMILIES (beyond kagome/Lieb/moiré)

### 🥇 #1 — BISMUTHATE / ANTIMONATE perovskites (Ba1-xKxBiO3 · (Ba,K)SbO3) — REAL off-diagonal SC, the grounding check

- **Material status: REAL, high-Tc.** Ba1-xKxBiO3 Tc≈30 K (classic, 1988). (Ba,K)SbO3 = NEW (2021,
  Nat. Mater. 2022; arXiv:2107.06596) Tc up to 15 K — but at comparable K-doping >2× the bismuthate Tc,
  and a LIGHTER bond (Sb-O vs Bi-O → higher Ω). Both are 3-dimensional perovskites with real metallic carriers
  once CDW is doped away.
- **Why it fits off-diagonal bond-Peierls — TEXTBOOK.** The parent is NOT charge-disproportionated
  (6s²+6s⁰); it is BOND-LENGTH disproportionated: 6s²+6s²L² (L = O 2p ligand hole), driven by the
  Bi/Sb–O bond-STRETCHING breathing phonon coupling via MODULATION OF THE M–O HOPPING INTEGRAL.
  That modulation-of-t IS the off-diagonal (SSH-type) coupling. This is the canonical real-world
  "negative-U / bond bipolaron" superconductor — the empirical sibling of the campaign's mechanism.
  (npj Comput. Mater. s41524-023-00998-6 explicitly models Bi 6s + O 2pσ + Bi-O bond-stretch optical
   phonon via Bi-O hopping modulation; arXiv:2408.00401 "small polarons persist into the SC doping range";
   Nat. Commun. s41467-023-36348-9 local inversion-breaking Bi-O phonon.)
- **Geometric / flat-band amenability — YES, the 3D analog.** The hole pair localizes on a SYMMETRIC
  combination of the 6 O-2pσ orbitals around a Bi site = a localized MOLECULAR-ORBITAL state — the 3D
  real-space-localized analog of a flat-band Wannier orbital. The breathing CDW is a 3D PEIERLS condensation
  of well-nested bands (same physics that makes pyrochlore/kagome flat bands). The bismuthate ⟨g⟩ is
  KNOWN to be large (λ≈1, Tc 30K at modest DOS) — i.e. high-⟨g⟩ is empirically demonstrated, the campaign's
  open question (does localization give high ⟨g⟩?) is answered YES here.
- **LIGHT bond Ω: ✓✓.** Breathing mode 570 cm⁻¹ ≈ 70 meV (Bi-O); Sb-O HIGHER. O-2pσ bond phonon = light.
- **Underexplored for the GEOMETRIC route: ✓.** Bismuthate SC is 35 yr old, but it is almost NEVER framed
  as flat-band-geometric-stiffness SC — the quantum-geometry/D_s∝⟨g⟩ lens (arXiv:2308.08248 etc.) has
  NOT been applied to the localized O-2pσ molecular-orbital band of Ba1-xKxBiO3 / (Ba,K)SbO3. That cross
  is the NOVEL angle: treat the bismuthate's localized molecular orbital as a geometric flat-band channel
  and compute D_s ∝ ⟨g⟩ — does the geometric Tc track the known 30K? If yes → mechanism validated on a
  REAL high-Tc material; if Sb-O lighter bond → predict ENHANCED geometric Tc (testable Δ).

### 🥈 #2 — β-PYROCHLORE 5d oxides (CsW2O6 · Cd2Re2O7) — SOC-flat-band + 3D Peierls, real metals

- **Material status: REAL metals/SC.** CsW2O6 = β-pyrochlore 5d (W 5d¹), metal-insulator transition,
  PERFECT flat band from strong SOC. Cd2Re2O7 = pyrochlore 5d (Re), REAL superconductor Tc≈1 K with a
  structural (Peierls-like) transition. CaNi2 = pyrochlore metal with 3D flat bands (Nature 2023).
- **Why it fits off-diagonal bond-Peierls.** CsW2O6's insulating state is a RARE 3D PEIERLS transition
  (simultaneous condensation of 3 density waves, W3-trimer formation = BOND reorganization), NOT Mott /
  dimer-singlet. The trimerization is bond-order driven — an off-diagonal (bond-modulating) instability.
  Cd2Re2O7's structural transition is a bond/Peierls distortion of the Re-Re network.
- **Geometric / flat-band: ✓✓ (this is the purest 3D flat band).** SOC generates MULTIPLY DEGENERATE
  PERFECT flat bands; electrons localize via spin-selective destructive interference (SU(2) gauge field) —
  a geometric flat band by construction. The high-T (undistorted) phase, if stabilized, was predicted to
  host an exotic eg+ieg SC. So a flat-band-geometric SC channel is LITERALLY the proposed ground state.
  (Nat. Commun. s41467-022-28132-y; arXiv:1606.02403; Nat. Commun. s41467-020-16873-7 W3 trimer.)
- **Bond Ω: ⚠ heavier.** W-O / Re-O bond phonons, but the metal is heavy 5d → lower Ω than O-2pσ-dominated
  bismuthate. The flat band is purer but the bond is heavier → ⟨g⟩·Ω tradeoff. Carriers exist (metallic).
- **Underexplored for geometric route: ✓✓ (very).** CsW2O6's predicted eg+ieg flat-band SC has NEVER
  been pushed into a bond-Peierls-⟨g⟩ computation; it is treated as a correlation/SOC problem, not an
  e-ph geometric one. Open lane.

### 🥉 #3 — XPd5 kagome (Ca/Sr/Ba) — COEXISTING electron + PHONON flat bands, brand-new (2025)

- **Material status: REAL, newly reported superconductor (May 2025, npj Comput. Mater. s41524-025-01815-y;
  arXiv:2505.14223).** CaPd5: electronic flat band (bandwidth <10 meV) sitting PRECISELY at E_F in the
  kz=0 plane, topologically nontrivial (Z2=1), PLUS a PHONON flat band from the same kagome geometric
  frustration (out-of-plane vibrations, destructive interference).
- **Why it fits off-diagonal bond-Peierls.** The phonon flat band is a geometric-frustration bond mode;
  the COEXISTENCE of an electronic flat band AT E_F with a phononic flat band is described by the authors
  as "a rare platform for fermion-boson (e-ph) interaction." That is precisely a high-⟨g⟩ flat-on-flat
  coupling — and the geometric kagome out-of-plane mode modulates Pd-Pd bonds (off-diagonal flavor).
- **Geometric / flat-band: ✓✓✓ (best E_F alignment of the lot).** Unlike CoSn (FB 80-100 meV BELOW E_F),
  CaPd5's flat band is AT E_F with REAL carriers — this directly fixes the campaign's #1 wall (carrier
  channel). Topological Z2 flat band → nonzero quantum metric → geometric D_s live.
- **Bond Ω: ⚠ Pd-Pd heavy.** Pd is a heavy 4d metal → the bond phonon is NOT light (no C/B/N/O bond).
  This caps the BCS-style Tc but the geometric route does not need light Ω as hard (D_s∝⟨g⟩, not Ω).
- **Underexplored: ✓✓✓ (months old).** No geometric-stiffness analysis exists yet — wide-open NOVEL lane,
  and it FIXES the carrier-at-E_F problem that broke CoSn/Nb3Cl8/COF.
