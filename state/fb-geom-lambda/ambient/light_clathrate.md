## 0. Headline verdict (read first)

**NO light-element covalent/clathrate superconductor credibly approaches 293 K at 1 atm.**
The honest ambient ceiling is **~100–130 K (predicted)**, and the best *measured* ambient
light-element value is **~11 K** (boron-doped diamond). The deciding calc is Gao et al.,
*Nat. Commun.* 16 (2025) — a screen of >20,000 metals' electron–phonon coupling that derives a
**practical ambient ceiling of ~100–120 K** from an inherent **ω_log ↔ λ trade-off**, concluding
room-temperature conventional superconductivity at ambient pressure is "extremely unlikely."
The ~293 K numbers all live at **110–140 GPa** (Y-Lu hydrides), not at 1 atm.

## 1. B–C / B–C–N clathrates (doped diamond-like B-C cages) — STRONGEST family

coupling to B–C bond-stretching modes** (the MgB2 mechanism, extended to a 3D cage).

**Best credible 1-atm B–C clathrate = KRbB₆C₆, Tc ≈ 102 K @ 0 GPa** (anharmonically stabilized,
SSCHA + ML potential — the most rigorous stability treatment available). SrB₃C₃ is the only one
**actually synthesized** (ambient-existing, ~44 K). Note the important caveat surfaced by the
2025/2026 anharmonic re-analyses ([arXiv 2512.05497](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.05497v1),
[npj 2025](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41524-025-01816-x)): many earlier 70–115 K predictions
rested on the *harmonic* approximation, which **breaks down** for these soft cages — quantum/anharmonic
effects are decisive for both stability and Tc. The honest ambient peak for this family is **~100 K**,
not the higher pressurized values.

**Adjacent B–C–N:** H-doped cubic-BN (P4̄3m-HB₄N₄) — predicted **Tc ≈ 122 K @ 0 GPa**, dynamically
stable at 1 atm, λ = 1.98 via H–N hybridization. [PMC11600296 / Adv. Sci. 2024](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11600296/).
This is the **single highest credibly-ambient light-element prediction found** — but still predicted-only.

## 2. Doped diamond / graphane / covalent sp³ — high Ω, measured but low Tc

Carrier channel = hole-doped C–C σ band; very high ω_log (diamond optical phonon ~1300 cm⁻¹).

| H-doped diamond | 117 K | (pressurized) | predicted | [ScienceDirect S2542529323001517](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S2542529323001517) |
| B-doped Q-carbon | ~36 K | ambient | measured (amorphous) | [ACS Nano](https://pubs.acs.org/doi/abs/10.1021/acsnano.7b01294) |

**The diamond Tc ≈ 290 K prediction directly touches the 293 K target — but it is the textbook example
of a theory-vs-experiment chasm.** It assumes ideal, defect-free, uniformly high B-doping into the σ band.
After >20 years of synthesis, measured Tc has never exceeded ~11 K (some onset claims to 25 K); boron
solubility, multiboron complexes, segregation, and vacancies cap the active carrier concentration far
below what the prediction needs. The 290 K figure is an *upper bound on an unrealizable idealization*, not
a credible ambient candidate. p-doped graphane (>90 K, 1% doping) is the more physical ambient sp³ number,
still predicted-only and tied to a delicate Kohn-anomaly enhancement.

## 6. DEPLETION TEST

- **Best ambient light-element candidate:** H-doped cubic boron nitride (P4̄3m-HB₄N₄), with KRbB₆C₆
  (B–C clathrate) the best *clathrate-specific* one.
- **Predicted Tc:** ~122 K (c-BN, μ*=0.1) / ~102 K (KRbB₆C₆ anharmonic) — both **predicted, not measured**.
- **Gap to 293 K:** **≈ 171 K** (from 122 K). For the best *measured* ambient light-element SC
  (~11 K B-diamond) the gap is **~282 K**.
- **Deciding calc:** **Gao et al., "The maximum Tc of conventional superconductors at ambient pressure,"
  Nat. Commun. 16 (2025)** ([article](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-025-63702-w) ·
  [arXiv 2502.18281](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2502.18281) · [PMC12423309](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12423309/)).
  Screening >20,000 metals' el-ph coupling, it derives a **practical ambient ceiling ~100–120 K** from an
  inherent **ω_log ↔ λ trade-off**: the Tc-maximizing Eliashberg function (single ultra-high-frequency mode
  with huge λ) is *unphysical* — you cannot simultaneously have ω_log ≈ 1800 K and λ ≈ 2–3 in a
  lattice-stable real material. Best theoretical approach to that limit = Li₂AuH₆ (116 K Eliashberg /
  91 K SCDFT), Li₂AgH₆ (109 K / 83 K). Room-T at ambient = "extremely unlikely." The ~283–288 K
  Y-Lu-H records all sit at **110–140 GPa**, confirming the high-Tc/high-P coupling.
