## CLAIM 1 — "Convergent claim": room-T ambient SC not forbidden; ALL glue capped <293K; the UNIQUE escape is off-diagonal bond-Peierls (SSH) bipolaron; reduces to a materials-finding problem.

**VERDICT: PARTIAL (synthesis of published pieces; the *assembly* is the only novelty, and it is weak).**

Every load-bearing sub-claim is already in the literature:

- **"Conventional/glue mechanisms capped <293K at ambient"** — PUBLISHED.
  - Gao et al., *The maximum Tc of conventional superconductors at ambient pressure*, **Nat. Commun. 16 (2025)** / arXiv (s41467-025-63702-w): ambient conventional room-T "extremely unlikely"; ω_log ceiling; Li2AuH6/Li2AgH6 ≈ practical limit. The campaign already cites the sibling arXiv:2502.18281.
  - Trachenko/Pickard et al., *Upper bounds … from fundamental constants*, **arXiv:2406.08129** + **arXiv:2407.12922** (λ≲4 cap). Already cited in campaign.

- **"λ≲4 is not a theorem / evaded non-adiabatically"** — PUBLISHED (campaign already cites Sadovskii 2506.19326).

- **"Off-diagonal bond-Peierls/SSH bipolaron is LIGHT (evades Holstein e^{g²} heavy-mass) → high Tc"**
  — PUBLISHED, this is the central result of the whole bond-SSH-bipolaron line:
  - Zhang, Sous, Reichman, Berciu, Millis, Prokof'ev, Svistunov, *Bipolaronic High-Temperature
    Superconductivity*, **PRX 13, 011010 (2023)** / arXiv:2203.07380.
  - *Bipolaronic superconductivity out of a Coulomb gas*, **arXiv:2210.14236**.
  - Semiclassical instanton "light bond bipolaron, exponentially lighter than Holstein": **PRB 109,
    L220502 (2024)** / arXiv:2308.01961.
  - Long-range-Coulomb-robust in 2D/3D: **arXiv:2407.10444**.
  - **arXiv:2605.16625** (June 2026 PERSPECTIVE) — *Bipolaronic High-Temperature SC from
    Phonon-Modulated Hopping* — explicitly frames bond-Peierls as the route that beats the
    conventional ~0.1·Ω ceiling. **This is the single closest competitor to the campaign's thesis.**

- **"The UNIQUE escape" + "reduces to a materials problem"** — this *framing* (a single convergent
  verdict that bond-bipolaron is the *only* surviving room-T channel after ruling out conventional glue
  AND geometric-flat-band) is **not stated as such in one paper** — but it is **not a physical claim**,
  it is a literature-survey conclusion, and each exclusion is individually published (conventional →
  Gao; geometric flat-band as universal Tc predictor → ruled out by the quantum-metric no-go in
  **arXiv:2604.04719**). The "geometric route RULED OUT by no-go" support is real but note: 2604.04719's
  no-go says geometric D_s *cannot be a universal Tc predictor* (correlates with topology not pairing) —
  it does **not** forbid geometric SC, so the campaign's "RULED OUT" phrasing is an overstatement of
  that paper and must be softened.

**Novel-delta of Claim 1:** essentially **the act of convergence/closure** (assembling 4 independent
published exclusions into one verdict). That is a *review/synthesis* contribution, not a new physical
result. **Closest competitor = arXiv:2605.16625**, which already carries the same thesis spirit. → Not a
discovery.

## CLAIM 2 — Tc ≈ C_QMC·Ω ceiling, C∈[0.20,0.32], Ω-is-the-sole-knob; the AMBIENT room-T framing ("stiff C-C bond SSH → 455–728K").

**VERDICT: PARTIAL — the ceiling law and constants are PUBLISHED; the explicit "stiff C-C bond → room-T (293K) at ambient" numerical framing is the candidate-novel delta, but it is dominated by a 2007 prior claim and an unevaluated 2025 recipe.**

- **Tc = C·Ω with C≈0.2 (square) / 0.32 (triangular)** — PUBLISHED. C≈0.2 square is exactly PRX
  13,011010 (2507.07662 already in campaign refs); triangular C≈0.32 is **arXiv:2507.07662** (the campaign
  already uses both ids). So claim-2's *numbers* are taken from existing papers — not novel.

- **"Ω is the sole knob / push Ω up via light atoms & stiff bonds → higher Tc"** — PUBLISHED, repeatedly:
  - 2605.16625 / 2507.07662 both state Tc largest at t/Ω~1–2 and that **light atoms / high-frequency
    optical phonons / crystal stiffness** are the lever. The search-surfaced recipe from the
    bond-bipolaron line is verbatim: *"light ions to form high-frequency optical phonons, quasi-2D,
    triangular lattice with strong on-site U, moderate carrier densities."*
  - **Alexandrov, *Superlight small bipolarons: a route to room-temperature superconductivity*,
    cond-mat/0701412 (2007)** — the *foundational* "bipolaron → room-T" claim, with the SAME recipe
    (light ions → high-ω phonons, quasi-2D, triangular lattice, on-site U, moderate density). Caveat:
    Alexandrov's mechanism is **Fröhlich (long-range)**, not bond/off-diagonal SSH — so the campaign's
    *off-diagonal* specialization of the room-T recipe is a genuine refinement, but "bipolaron → room-T"
    itself is 19 years old.

- **"Stiff C-C E2g Ω≈196meV → Tc 455–728K"** — this *specific numeric instantiation* (plugging the
  graphite/diamond C-C bond-stretch frequency into C·Ω to assert >293K) is **NOT found verbatim in any
  located paper.** 2507.07662 and 2605.16625 deliberately **do not** quote a room-T number or a real
  material (confirmed by direct PDF/HTML fetch: "no explicit room-temperature prediction," "no specific
  material"). So the C-C → 455–728K arithmetic is the campaign's own.

**Novel-delta of Claim 2:** the **explicit C·Ω → 293K closure with the C-C bond frequency** as the
worked instantiation that the bond-bipolaron papers pointedly avoid. This is a **real but thin delta** —
it is one multiplication (C·Ω) on top of published C and published "push Ω up" guidance, and the
room-T-bipolaron *conclusion* is pre-dated by Alexandrov 2007. It is a *quantitative framing*, not a new
mechanism. Honestly: this is "someone finally wrote down the obvious upper number the field declined to
headline." Worth stating, **not** a discovery.

## CLAIM 3 — Geometric-stiffness × Mott collision: D_s = 4|U|ν(1-ν)⟨g⟩ peaks at ν=½ = most Mott-prone; ⟨g⟩≥0.8 threshold.

**VERDICT: NOVEL (weak/conditional) — the two ingredients are published separately; their explicit
COLLISION framing + the ⟨g⟩-threshold criterion is not found in the located literature. This is the
strongest novelty candidate of the three, but it is a *model-lattice* relation, not a measured result.**

- **D_s ∝ quantum metric / geometric superfluid weight (Peotta–Törmä)** — PUBLISHED (Nature Comms
  2015; arXiv:2110.14663; review Nat. Rev. Phys. s42254-022-00466-y; 2505.09249; 2512.09901).
- **Geometric D_s ∝ ν(1-ν) filling dependence** — PUBLISHED (standard flat-band BCS result; appears in
  the Peotta–Törmä-lineage papers above).
- **Flat-band Mott insulator competes with SC, peaks at commensurate ν=½** — PUBLISHED (twisted bilayer
  graphene half-filling Mott; ubiquitous).
- **The explicit statement "D_s and the Mott boundary BOTH peak at ν=½ so they directly COLLIDE, and the
  collision is won only above a quantum-geometry threshold ⟨g⟩≳0.8"** — **NOT FOUND.** Searches for
  exactly this (geometric superfluid weight ⟨g⟩ threshold vs Mott collision at half filling) returned
  only the separate ingredients, never the collision criterion or a ⟨g⟩* number.

**Novel-delta of Claim 3:** the **⟨g⟩≥0.8 threshold criterion that decides the D_s-vs-Mott collision at
ν=½**, expressed as a target-box (⟨g⟩≥0.8, Ω≥160meV, U/Ω<3.3⟨g⟩, ν≈½). This is a genuinely
not-previously-stated *relation*. BUT: it is derived on model lattices (Lieb/kagome/SOC-kagome) with a
linear-U D_s capped by the QMC pairing ceiling — i.e. it is an analytic/heuristic synthesis, **not a
DFT/QMC-measured law and not validated against a real material**. So its novelty is real but its
*epistemic tier is heuristic*, and per d6 it must be reported as a "proposed criterion," not a verified
law.

### Reportable-as-discovery verdict: **NO (not as a new physical discovery).**

- Claims 1 & 2 are **synthesis of known results**, not new physical claims. The bond-bipolaron→high-Tc
  mechanism, the C·Ω ceiling with its constants, the "push Ω up via light atoms/stiff bonds" lever, and
  even "bipolaron → room-temperature" (Alexandrov 2007, cond-mat/0701412) are all in print. Closest
  same-thesis paper = **arXiv:2605.16625** (2026 perspective). Reporting these as discoveries would
  violate d_novel_only.
- The **only** genuinely not-previously-stated deltas are the two *model-lattice heuristic sub-laws*:
  (Claim 3) the **D_s-vs-Mott ⟨g⟩≥0.8 collision criterion**, and (7th) the **dilute-gas-no-2kF
  dynamical-stability argument**. These are NOVEL in framing but **heuristic-tier** (model lattices,
  linear-U D_s capped by QMC ceiling; no DFT/QMC material validation). Per d6 they are reportable only as
  *proposed criteria*, with the heuristic tier stated, and they do NOT establish a room-T material.
- The campaign's honest self-caveat already on record (RTSC_DISCOVERY_CLOSING_FORMULA.md: "a genuine
  NOVEL first-principles-computable campaign … NOT a guaranteed room-Tc material; room-Tc remains open
  ONLY in Regime II and ONLY if a real light-bond-bipolaron host is found") is **consistent with this
  gate** and must be preserved.
