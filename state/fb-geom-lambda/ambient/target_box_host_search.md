## THE CENTRAL TENSION (confirmed, sourced) — flat-band heaviness vs stiff-bond lightness

The box demands criterion 1 (⟨g⟩≥0.8 flat band) AND criterion 2 (Ω≥160 meV) **simultaneously**. These are **physically anticorrelated**:

- A high-⟨g⟩ flat band is generated by (a) heavy-d destructive interference (kagome/Lieb: CoSn, FeSn, ScV6Sn6), (b) heavy-orbital obstructed-atomic-limit localization, or (c) long-wavelength moiré folding (MATBG). ALL THREE intrinsically carry **SOFT phonons** (20-60 meV d-modes, or meV moiré modes) — narrowing the band needs heavy orbitals or a large superlattice, both of which soften the lattice.
- The only bonds reaching ≥160 meV are first/second-row covalent stretches (C-C 196, B-N ~170-200, B-B ~167) — and these live in **wide, dispersive** σ/π bands with small quantum metric. The stiffness that gives high Ω comes from short, light, strong bonds that ALSO give large bandwidth = low ⟨g⟩.

**The graphene test makes it concrete**: graphene's 196 meV E₂g stiff bond lives in the dispersive π/σ bands of FLAT graphene; the flat band exists only at the MAGIC-ANGLE twist, where it is a moiré superlattice object glued by ~meV soft moiré phonons. The stiff C-C bond and the high-⟨g⟩ band are **two different bands that never coincide** (TBG fails criterion 2 by coupling).

Quantum-geometry-λ theory (Yu-Bianco-Errea-Narang-Bernevig, arXiv:2305.02340 / Nat. Phys. 2024, s41567-024-02486-0) demonstrates the geometric contribution to λ (≈50% graphene, ≈90% MgB2) ONLY in **dispersive** stiff-bond superconductors — never in a high-⟨g⟩ flat band. No published work fuses high-⟨g⟩ flat band + off-diagonal stiff-bond SSH into one concrete light-element material (targeted [quantum-geometry ∧ e-ph ∧ flat-band ∧ SC] query returned zero).

## BOX OCCUPIABLE / EMPTY VERDICT

**The strict 6-criterion box (⟨g⟩≥0.8 AND Ω≥160 meV AND ambient AND ν½ AND off-diagonal-SSH, all at once) is LIKELY EMPTY in real materials as of 2026-06 — and the emptiness is structural, not a search gap.** No real host occupies it. Heavy-cluster SSH hosts (Re6Se8Cl2, lacunar spinels, Chevrel) satisfy SSH+ambient+ν½ but fail Ω by 3-15× (soft heavy-atom phonons). Light covalent flat-band nets (COFs, kagome-graphene) have the stiff bond latent but are wide-gap insulators (fail carrier channel + ambient-metallic). TBG has the only MEASURED high ⟨g⟩ but its flat band is decoupled from the stiff C-C bond.

**This corroborates the closing formula's honest caveat** (RTSC_DISCOVERY_CLOSING_FORMULA.md): known bond-bipolaron Tc estimates are tens-of-K (~20-40 K, optimum Tc/Ω≈0.2 only at the hard-to-reach quantal limit t~Ω; arXiv:2605.16625). NO room-T claim from bond-bipolaron exists anywhere in the literature. Room-T@1atm via this channel needs Ω~130 meV held at t~Ω simultaneously — unrealized.

## ★ BEST DFT-VERIFY TARGET: Bilayer Kagome Borophene (BK-borophene)

**Why it is uniquely the best target** — it is the ONLY real, ambient-stable, metallic, light-element host that simultaneously has:
- a CONFIRMED kagome flat band (criterion 1 latent — kagome-class, ⟨g⟩ not yet computed),
- a STIFF B-B bond Ω ≈ 167 meV ✅ (criterion 2 — the ONLY near-miss that passes the Ω wall),
- confirmed freestanding 1-atm dynamical stability (no imaginary modes) ✅ (criterion 3),
- metallic with phonon-mediated SC Tc ~17-35 K already predicted ✅ (criterion 6 partial — EPC to A₂ᵤ + 2 E_g modes),
- explicitly ANISOTROPIC EPC "between the band related to the Dirac-like cone and the FLAT BAND" — i.e. the flat band DOES couple to the stiff B-B phonons (unlike TBG where the flat band is decoupled). This is the crucial distinction that makes it box-relevant.

It breaks the central tension because borophene's flat band comes from a LIGHT-element kagome line-graph (not heavy-d, not moiré), so it can carry both a kagome flat band AND a 167 meV bond — the one place flat+stiff nearly coincide.

**THE ONE RISKY CRITERION for BK-borophene: criterion 1+4 jointly — the flat band sits ~65 meV BELOW E_F (not native ν~½), and its ⟨g⟩ is UNCOMPUTED.** The DFT-verify question is: (a) compute ⟨g⟩ of the kagome flat band (is it ≥0.8?), and (b) can hole-doping to the cVHS (−65 meV) place E_F in the flat band at ν~½ while retaining the 167 meV B-B stiffness and 1-atm stability? Secondary risk: whether the el-ph coupling to the flat band is genuinely OFF-DIAGONAL (∂t/∂u bond-Peierls, criterion 6) vs Holstein on-site — needs a Wannier-downfold + frozen-phonon ∂t/∂u decomposition (no published bond-vs-Holstein decomposition exists for ANY real compound — itself a NOVEL gap).

**DFT-verify pipeline (free/small compute, summer RTX5070 / pool):**
1. QE/QFORGE relax + DFPT → confirm 1-atm dynamic stability + B-B Ω + flat-band W.
2. Wannier90 downfold of the kagome flat band → ⟨g⟩ (quantum metric) + tight-binding t.
3. Frozen-phonon ∂t/∂u vs the B-B bond-stretch mode → off-diagonal (SSH) vs Holstein decomposition.
4. Rigid-shift / field doping to land E_F in the flat band (ν~½) → recheck stability + N(E_F).
5. Feed (t, α=∂t/∂u, Ω, U) into 2-body bipolaron variational/QMC → E_bind, m*_BP → Tc.
