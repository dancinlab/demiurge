## KEY TEST 1 — affinity ≠ selectivity: CONFIRMED
Navitoclax = decisive counterexample: Ki ≤0.5 nM (tightest in corpus) yet NARROWEST window (on-target platelet thrombocytopenia, platelets depend solely on BCL-xL). Affinity → potency, NOT selectivity. SCAP-differential rank-orders window; affinity inverts it. AND-gated agents (Nav-Gal, UBX1325-local, uPAR CAR-T) widen window with SAME/lower-affinity warhead.

## KEY TEST 2 — ceiling (B) CONFIRMED · escape (C) PARTIAL
single-SCAP agents clear only 30-70% (D+Q); on-target tox is the signature (navitoclax→platelets; UBX0101→Ph2 OA FAIL). AND-gate/local widen WINDOW (Nav-Gal, UBX1325 intravitreal) but clinical efficacy escape UNPROVEN — UBX1325 Ph2b ASPIRE MISSED (non-inferiority only at 88% CI vs 90% pre-spec). Entire clinical corpus = Ph2 misses + feasibility, NO approved senolytic.
